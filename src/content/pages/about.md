## Bio

**Title:** You Know Me As Dubbz

**Content:** South Florida-based DJ **You Know Me As Dubbz** has earned a reputation for delivering high-energy performances and seamless mixes across a wide range of genres. Whether spinning at local venues, exclusive parties, or on international stages, Dubbz's dynamic approach to DJing keeps the crowd moving and creates an unforgettable atmosphere.

With over **10 years of professional experience**, the journey started in the South Florida scene and expanded to corporate events, luxury weddings, premier nightclubs, and festival stages. The signature sound blends house, hip-hop, afrobeats, dancehall, top 40, and latin — curated for each unique room.
